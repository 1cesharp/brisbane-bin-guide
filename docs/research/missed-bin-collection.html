Missed bin collection — what to do in Brisbane

Updated 2026-08-30 · sources checked at build time

A missed bin collection is one of the most common bin complaints in Brisbane — and reports have been rising: "missed bin reports have been exploding in Brisbane each year since 2022. Basics aren't being done" [1].

What to do, in order

  1. Check the calendar first. Public holidays shift collection days by one

(or to the next service day). Confirm your day on your council's bin-day page.

  1. Check placement. Bin out by the night before, wheels to the kerb, 30cm+

gap from cars/other bins, lid closed, not overfilled. Overfull or blocked bins can legally be skipped.

  1. Report it to your council the same day. All SEQ councils take missed-bin

reports online or by phone. The cutoff matters: report within ~24-48h of collection day and crews usually come back within a day or two; later reports can mean waiting until your next scheduled collection.

  1. If it keeps happening, log each report — councils use repeat reports to

fix route problems with their contractors.

Why it spikes

Contractor shortages, route changes after FOGO rollouts, and post-holiday volume all drive missed collections [1] [2].
