Missed bin collection — what to do in Brisbane

Updated 2026-08-31 · sources checked at build time

If Brisbane City Council missed your bin, the reporting window depends on the bin type. First check that the truck had a fair chance to service the bin, then use Council's official report form.

What to do, in order

  1. Check the collection day and placement. Bins are collected every day of the year, including public holidays. Put bins at the kerb by 5.30am, at least 30cm apart, with lids closed, wheels facing your property and no parked-car or pathway obstruction [1].
  2. Check common reasons for a missed collection. Council says a bin may not have been collected if it exceeded the 70 kilogram weight limit, was obstructed, or was put out after the truck had already serviced the address [2].
  3. Report it within the correct window. Report a red-lid bin after 4.30pm on collection day. Report a yellow or green bin within 2 working days of the collection day. Council says missed bins will be collected as soon as possible [1].
  4. Use the official action now: Report a bin problem online → or phone Brisbane City Council on 07 3403 8888 [1].

If the problem repeats, keep the report reference and note the date, bin colour and placement conditions. That gives Council useful detail when you follow up.
