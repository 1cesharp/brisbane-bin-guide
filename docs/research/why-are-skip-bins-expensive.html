Why is hiring a skip bin so expensive?

Updated 2026-08-30 · sources checked at build time

Skip bin hire looks like "paying for a big steel box", so the price feels inflated. In reality six costs stack up:

  1. Landfill disposal is charged by weight. The hire covers delivery and pickup;

disposal is metered at the tip gate. Queensland landfill levies make the per-tonne gate fee a large share of the total [1].

  1. Weight, not volume, is the cliff. Bins have tonne allowances; concrete, soil

and tile loads blow through them quickly. Heavy-waste bins cost more than general-waste bins of the same size [2].

  1. Levies differ by council area and facility. Where your junk is weighed and

dumped changes the gate price, which is why identical bins can differ across a council border [1].

  1. Fuel and truck time. One delivery + one pickup trip per job, with urban

congestion pricing in the labour.

  1. Permits. If the bin sits on the street or nature strip, the council charges

a permit fee on top — Brisbane included [3].

  1. Demand spikes. Pre-Christmas and pre-storm-season weeks see prices firm up.

How to pay less: sort heavy material out of general waste, pick the smallest bin you can realistically fill (overfilling = over-weight charges), keep the bin on your own property to skip the permit, and compare at least two providers.
