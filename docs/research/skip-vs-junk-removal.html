Skip bin vs junk removal service

Updated 2026-08-31 · sources checked at build time

Two ways to clear junk: a skip bin you fill yourself, or a junk removal crew that loads and takes it away the same visit.

Skip bin Junk removal service
You do the loadingYesNo — crew loads
Typical cost driverSize (m³) + waste typeVolume of items + labour time
Best forRenovations, gradual cleanups, heavy loads staged over daysSingle-room cleanouts, furniture, same-day urgency
TimingBin sits for days (hire period)One visit, usually 1–2 hours
Heavy materialFine (within weight limits)Often priced at premium
Space neededDriveway or (permitted) street spotJust a truck park

Rule of thumb: junk removal wins on speed and zero labour; a skip wins on price per cubic metre once you're above a ute-tray of stuff, and for anything heavy. Get both quotes if your job is either very small or very large — the crossover point surprises people.
