Where does your skip bin waste actually go?

Updated 2026-08-31 · sources checked at build time

Your bin leaves on a truck and the load is weighed and sorted at a transfer station or landfill gate — the same weighbridge that generates the disposal charge on your invoice [1].

  • General waste mostly goes to landfill; some facilities recover metals and

timber first [2].

  • Green waste is usually composted or mulched rather than landfilled, and is

cheaper to dispose of — which is why green-waste bins are cheaper to hire [1].

  • Concrete, brick, soil go to construction-and-demolition recyclers where

available; clean loads avoid contamination fees.

  • Contaminated loads (general waste mixed into a green-waste bin, or hazardous

items anywhere) attract surcharges or rejection at the gate.

Cost lever: separating green waste and heavy inert material before pickup is the single biggest way to avoid gate-fee surprises.
