About this site

Updated 2026-08-31 · sources checked at build time

What this is. A small, free reference for skip bin sizes, council permits and indicative prices around Brisbane. It is rebuilt nightly by an automated agent, which re-checks the council pages in the source register and refreshes the build date.

How to read the numbers. Facts about permits and rules link to the official council page. Price bands are indicative market ranges compiled at build time — always confirm the final price with the provider, since weight limits and waste type change the total.

Money. Some outbound links may later earn a commission if you book through them. That never changes the data: rules and permit facts come from councils, not from advertisers.

Contact. Corrections welcome — the site is maintained as part of a small automation experiment and can be reached via the operator's contact on file.

Some links may earn us a commission. We only list providers we have verified data for, and price/permit facts are always free to read.
